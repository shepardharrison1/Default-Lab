## Notes on evidence usage

- Vendor pages are used to establish product existence, product claims, APIs, pricing pages, and positioning. They do not independently prove adoption, revenue, customer retention, or willingness to pay.
- The arXiv API-drift paper supports the narrower claim that current external documentation improves coding outcomes but does not fully solve evolving API failures. It does **not** prove that proprietary-data brokerage is a company.
- The Cloudflare/Human Native date is deliberately documented as inconsistent: the Cloudflare URL/category, BusinessWire URL, and search metadata indicate 2026-01-15, while the body text observed on Cloudflare's press page says January 15, 2025. This pack treats the acquisition announcement as 2026-01-15 and records the 2025 body date as a likely typo.
- Local prior-pack cross-references are supporting research artifacts, not external evidence.
