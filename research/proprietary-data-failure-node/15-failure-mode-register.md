# Failure-Mode Register

**Research date:** 2026-07-16  
**Posture:** Skeptical; assume the thesis fails unless a narrow pilot proves otherwise.  
**Recommendation tie-in:** **Pursue only through a narrow pilot.**

## Bottom line

The company thesis has many ways to fail. The most dangerous pattern is not technical impossibility; it is that the technically impressive version becomes an infrastructure feature inside coding-agent hosts, observability platforms, cloud data marketplaces, or data-owner APIs before a standalone company earns durable margin. The second most dangerous pattern is that the true repeatable need is not "buy proprietary data at an agent failure node" but "vertical software, integrations, and expert workflow validation."

The narrow pilot should therefore be built to falsify, not to validate. It must prove that a specific vertical failure node is detectably data-caused, that licensed minimal access resolves it better than free substitutes and direct/BYO access, that buyers pay enough above source cost, and that rights holders accept the leakage and legal controls.

## Fatality summary

**Fatal to horizontal founding strategy:** FM-01, FM-05, FM-08, FM-10, FM-11, FM-12, FM-18, FM-30, FM-34. These point to the coding-agent-specific detection layer becoming an infrastructure feature and the broad marketplace being commoditized by platforms, direct APIs, and open payment/tooling rails.

**Fatal to any company if unresolved:** FM-13, FM-14, FM-17, FM-28, FM-29. These attack legal rights, supply willingness, data-owner moat preservation, and trust.

**Likely to reframe into vertical software/services:** FM-20, FM-21, FM-33. These may still produce revenue, but not the claimed broker marketplace.

## Pilot gating implications

The pilot must reject the thesis if any of the following happen:

1. Fewer than 70% of triggered events are confirmed by expert review as data-caused in the chosen vertical.
2. Licensed minimal access does not beat free/public/direct/BYO-source baselines by a material margin.
3. The buyer will not pre-commit to a price above source cost for renewal.
4. The rights holder will not sign agent-use, no-training, no-redistribution, retention, and audit terms.
5. The workflow requires broad corpus access or repeated enumeration that the rights holder views as moat-destroying.

The failure register supports only one recommendation: **Pursue only through a narrow pilot.**
