## Evidence grading

- **Verified** — primary company, product, standards, regulatory, court, SEC, or official documentation source, or a directly cited public filing/document.
- **Company-claimed** — primary company/press material that supports the claim as a company statement, but does not independently prove revenue, usage volume, or product-market fit.
- **Secondary** — reputable journalism, analyst/legal commentary, or trade coverage used where primary sources do not disclose the relevant detail.
- **Inference** — conclusions drawn in the research pack from the cited evidence; inferences are not treated as facts in this bibliography.

Unless a row is marked **secondary** or **company-claimed**, the entry is treated as a verified primary/authoritative source for the narrow phrase in "What it supports."
